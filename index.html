Meet the designer

Now, alongside an MSc in Information Technology at the University of Eastern Finland, I design those lessons on purpose.

Frame 01Delivered · revised

City Traffic Signal

K-12 coding lesson · Grades 4–8, mixed ability · Curriculum Developer application

Learners & problem
A mixed class: some have never coded, others are ready for timing and logic. One lesson has to stretch both.
Objective
Learners will build a working traffic-light sequence and explain how timing and conditions control it.
Design moves
A real-world anchor every child recognises · tiered challenges · deck revised after reviewer feedback.

Frame 03In progress

Scratch Level 1 Curriculum

Curriculum Developer · live online coding programme · ages 7–12

Learners
Children new to coding in live online classes, with parents who want to see progress.
Framework
Inquiry-based (inspired by IB PYP/MYP principles), four age stages, 48 lessons per level.
Design moves

Frame 04In progress · MSc thesis

Hands-Free Scratch

Accessibility research · University of Eastern Finland

Problem
Block coding needs a mouse and fine motor control, which shuts out many children with motor disabilities.
Approach
Program Scratch with eye gaze and voice instead of drag-and-drop.
LXD skills
Inclusive design · user research · usability evaluation.
Artefacts
GitHub repo ↗Proposal
